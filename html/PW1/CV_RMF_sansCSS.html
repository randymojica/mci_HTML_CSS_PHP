About Me

A M.Sc. student coursing the second year of the EMJM ChEMoinformatics+ programme, having completed my Bachelor in Chemistry, and carrying more than six years of professional experience, with a robust background in organic and computational chemistry. I am honored to have been awarded an excellence scholarship fully funded by the National Secretariat for Science, Technology, and Innovation of Panama (SENACYT) to pursue a doctoral degree in Chemistry at the University of Strasbourg, in France. This scholarship covers the tuition fees for preparing a Master's degree beforehand the doctoral program, and the subsequent living expenses. I excel in the intersection of chemical analysis and computational techniques. My expertise spans from molecular modeling of relevant biological systems to natural products-based drug discovery, underpinned by a strong foundation in theoretical and experimental techniques in both chemistry and biotechnology. I am a proactive contributor to the scientific community, with multiple publications and presentations at national and international conferences. My collaborative spirit is evidenced by the multiple scholarships I have been awarded, reflecting my commitment to scientific excellence, allowing me to work with esteemed researchers and displaying an active participation in academic activities for sharing the developed scientific knowledge to the public.
